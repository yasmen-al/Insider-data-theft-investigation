# Scenario

## Background
Caleb Bridger is a sales analyst at a fictional company Jewellery Dream. Caleb left the company a week ago to join a rival jewellery company. Before Resigning, Caleb Bridger decides to take the customer list and the pricing of the jewellery to gain potential customers.

After Caleb leaves, the manager of Jewellery Dream notices that a customer has been approached by Caleb's new company. HR asks the forensics examiner to examine Caleb's returned laptop.


## Scope
- The "laptop" is a Windows 10 Pro VM that I built, so I am both the suspect and the examiner.
- All data is made up so no personal data was used.
- No USB device was used.
- OneDrive account sign up was blocked and I didn't try to get around it.
- Only the "laptop" disk was examined.


## Simulated Activity
- 10/10/2026 - Created the "laptop" VM. On the VM I created some files and produced normal browsing activity on Edge.
- 11/10/2026 - Opened both csv files and archived them into 'HolidayPictures.7z' to disguise the csv files as a pictures archive.
- The times come from the [Contemporaneous Notes](https://github.com/yasmen-al/Insider-data-theft-investigation/blob/main/lab-notebook/raw-notes/contemporaneous-notes.md)
