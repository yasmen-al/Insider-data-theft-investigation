# Insider-data-theft-investigation

# SCENARIO
Caleb Bridger is a sales analyst at a fictional company Jewellery Dream. Caleb left the company a week ago to join a rival jewellery company. Before Resigning, Caleb Bridger decides to take the customer list and the pricing of the jewellery to gain potential customers.

After Caleb leaves, the manager of Jewellery Dream notices that a customer has been approached by Caleb's new company. HR asks the forensics examiner to examine Caleb's returned laptop.
